## 📝 Detailed Descriptions

### 1. Portfolio Optimization
**📂 Location:** [`/Portfolio-Optimization`](./Portfolio-Optimization/)

* **Focus:** Portfolio selection when returns are given by a finite set of scenarios. The classical mean-variance model is the reference point; the project studies the risk measures that keep the problem linear — mean absolute deviation, semideviation, the minimax rule, Gini's mean difference and CVaR — and the mixed-integer models that follow from realistic constraints such as cardinality, minimum buy-in, minimum lots and transaction costs.
* **Tutor:** Justo Puerto Albandoz, Head of the Department of Statistics and Operations Research. **Supervision:** Alberto Torrejón Valenzuela.
* **Context:** Research assistant position at the Department of Statistics and Operations Research, University of Seville, since October 2026 (2026-27 academic year).
* **Companion code:** [`Portfolio_Data_Reconstruction.ipynb`](../Python_Tests/Portfolio_Data_Reconstruction.ipynb).

### 2. Warehouse Slotting Optimization
**📂 Location:** [`/Warehouse-Slotting-Optimization`](./Warehouse-Slotting-Optimization/)

* **Focus:** Joint assignment of products to the storage channels of an automated picking system and to their manual replenishment zones, minimizing the operational effort of replenishment.
* **Supervisor:** Alberto Torrejón Valenzuela.
* **Institution:** Institute of Mathematics of the University of Seville (IMUS), July – September 2026.
* **Output:** A MILP formulation, a three-phase heuristic (greedy construction + Iterated Local Search) and a technical report. *(Confidential: no code, data or results are published.)*
